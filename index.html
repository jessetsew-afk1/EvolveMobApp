Why we built it

"How did I not know about this?"

As college students, we were tired of hearing about incredible events after they happened. That one question sparked Evolve.

We wanted to build a platform that would make it easy for people to find and create these can't-miss moments in real time, right in their backyard. A tool to transform FOMO into unforgettable nights and new adventures with amazing people.
